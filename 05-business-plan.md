# 05 · Business plan — Q4 2026 → 2029

[02](02-rd-runway.md) is the product plan; [03](03-business-model.md) is the model. This
document is the company on a timeline: what we intend to earn, hire, and spend over three
years, and — in keeping with how this room works — the **gates where the plan changes
course**, stated in advance. Figures are **targets, not forecasts**: they are the numbers
we resource against and measure ourselves by, not predictions we claim to know.

## Starting position (September 2026)

- **Product**: the engine and the full agent stack running continuously (see
  [01](01-core-technology.md)); the Bologna urban-twin pilot live and calibrated;
  methodology peer-reviewed; the OSS floor broad and maintained.
- **Revenue**: zero. Everything to date is built, not sold — deliberately: the room you
  are reading is part of changing that.
- **Team**: three co-founders, part-time-to-full-time mix; no employees.
- **Capital**: raising **$1.5M** (the [pitch deck](https://gaiawm.github.io/pitch-deck/)
  ask), allocated 50% engineering / 25% commercial / 25% research.

## Year 1 — Prove (Q4 2026 → end 2027)

The year has one job: turn running code into **paid evidence** in each market, however
small the invoices. Evidence, not revenue, is the Year-1 output; the revenue follows it.

**Games.** Execute runway Horizon 1: the evaluator sandbox, the public proof surfaces,
population scale. Commercial target: **2–3 design partnerships**, LOI-backed, at least
one of them paying for the pilot. The GDC-anchored motion from [03](03-business-model.md)
runs all year; the commercial lead (first hire) owns the list and the close.

**Urban twins.** Bologna graduates from pilot to **reference**: a public, periodically
updated calibration report on open data — the artifact a consultancy can put in front of
a city. Target: one paid build engagement through a consultancy partner, and **2–3 EU
proposals submitted** with consortia (Horizon-class calls fund exactly the build phase;
their decision cycles mean money submitted in 2027 lands in 2028 — we submit early
precisely because of that lag).

**Defence & disaster.** One **lighthouse exercise** with a civil-protection organisation:
scoped small, priced honestly (subsidised if the reference rights justify it), designed
from day one to be publishable. The deliverable that matters is the after-action data
package — it is the sales artifact for every exercise after it.

**Team.** First hires, in order: the **commercial lead** (the deck's open role — owns
games pipeline and the twin channel partnerships) and **one senior engineer** (population
scale and enterprise hardening). Founders stay the twins/defence delivery capacity this
year.

**Exit criteria for the year**: at least one signed design partnership, one paid twin
engagement, one delivered exercise. Three markets, three first invoices.

## Year 2 — Convert (2028)

The conversion year: everything piloted in 2027 either becomes recurring or gets
demoted — see the gates below.

**Games.** Design partners convert to **world licences** — the first real recurring
revenue line. The deck's 18-month milestones come due mid-year: **10 paying customers or
$1M ARR, 3 platform partnerships, 1000+ active API users**. Horizon 2's designer product
is what makes the licence renew: the creative-director surface, not the CTO demo.

**Urban twins.** Build engagements flow into **run licences**. The single most important
event of the year is the **first calibration-report renewal**: a city (or its
consultancy) paying a second year because the report proved useful is the falsifiable
test of the "calibration report is the SLA" thesis. EU-funded projects land and co-fund
the build teams.

**Defence & disaster.** The lighthouse becomes a **repeatable product**: the same
exercise shape delivered at 2–3 organisations, first **readiness licence** signed
(the standing twin between exercises). Begin the prime/integrator conversations with
delivered-exercise evidence rather than slideware.

**Team.** Grow to **6–8**: a twins delivery engineer (the consultancy-facing role), a
developer-relations/support engineer (the games funnel demands it by now), and a second
core engineer. Still one commercial lead; channels are how we scale sales before
salespeople.

**Funding decision point (mid-2028).** With the 18-month milestones measured, a
**Series A is optional**: we raise to accelerate a motion that is demonstrably working,
never to survive. The seed plus grant co-funding plus Year-2 revenue is planned to carry
the company into 2029 at base-case burn.

## Year 3 — Compound (2029)

**Games.** The first shipped title on the engine turns on **usage revenue** (per-NPC /
per-player) — revenue that grows with the customer's success rather than our sales
effort. Horizon 3's protocol stewardship makes the formats the default: every third-party
shell and client strengthens the licence, not the alternative.

**Urban twins.** The motion inverts: consultancies **resell** the build phase, we keep
the run licences and the ask-phase studies. Renewal rate is the KPI that matters; a twin
that renews twice is infrastructure, not a project.

**Defence & disaster.** Exercises booked through the channel without us in the room;
readiness licences at the organisations that exercised in 2028. Defence-proper
engagements only via primes holding the frameworks, inside the published
[dual-use guardrails](03-business-model.md).

**Team.** **10–13**: second commercial hire (channel management), an exercise programme
role, engineering to ~6. Still small on purpose — channels and usage carry the growth.

**Financial shape.** Base case: **default-alive** — recurring revenue covers burn without
the A. Targets, not forecasts: exit 2029 at **$2.5–4M ARR**, mixed roughly half games
licences+usage, a third twins run/ask, the balance exercises and readiness — with the mix
itself treated as a finding, not a commitment: one engine means we can follow whichever
market converts fastest without rework.

## Revenue logic (why these targets and not others)

- **Games** revenue is licence-led and lumpy early (a studio decision is slow but large);
  the 2028 $1M-ARR milestone assumes single-digit licences plus the long-tail funnel —
  consistent with letting three segments go ([03](03-business-model.md)).
- **Twins** revenue is arc-shaped by design: build (project, partly grant-funded) → run
  (recurring, ours) → ask (attach). The arc exists precisely so public money funds the
  expensive phase and private renewals fund us.
- **Defence & disaster** is event revenue converting to standing revenue; we plan it as
  the *slowest* line deliberately — procurement speed is not something a plan should
  pretend to control.
- **We never monetise inference** (BYOK) — no revenue line above carries token-price
  exposure, which is why the margin structure holds across all three markets.

## Gates and kill criteria (stated in advance)

| Gate | If it fails | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Mid-2027**: ≥1 LOI-backed games design partnership | Zero LOIs despite the sandbox and the GDC motion | Games demotes to funnel-and-OSS presence; commercial lead re-points at twins. The engine roadmap barely changes — that is what one-engine-three-markets buys |
| **End-2027**: one paid engagement per new vertical | Twins or defence produce no paying pilot | The failing vertical loses its 2028 hiring slot and runs founder-led and opportunistic until a partner or grant changes the evidence |
| **End-2028**: first calibration-report renewal | Twin customers take the build, skip the run | Twins reverts to a project business through consultancies (still profitable, no longer a recurring thesis); the run-licence pitch is rewritten from evidence |
| **End-2028**: repeat exercise booking within 12 months of the lighthouse | One-and-done | Defence & disaster stays a capability we sell opportunistically, with no dedicated headcount, until the channel produces demand |
| **Any point**: the academic branch requests headcount | — | It doesn't get it. Collaboration time, yes; a hire, no. It is leverage, not a line ([03](03-business-model.md)) |

## What this plan refuses to assume

No assumption that a model provider builds our layer for us, or against us — the
[category defence](03-business-model.md) holds either way. No assumption that any single
market carries the company: the plan survives any one of the three converting slowly,
and the gates say which lever moves when it does. No headcount ahead of evidence: every
2028 and 2029 hire is attached to a 2027 or 2028 gate having passed. And no revenue from
anything we said we would never do: inference, attention, the owner's data, or work
outside the dual-use guardrails.
